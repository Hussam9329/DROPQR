Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

DropQR has no accounts, so we don't know who you are. We keep only what is needed to run the service and stop abuse.

What we store

  • Your file, with its name, size and type, until it expires or runs out of downloads. It is not public: it can only be downloaded through its link.
  • Link details: when the file was shared, when it expires, and how many times it was downloaded.
  • A scrambled visitor code made from your IP address with a secret key (a one-way hash). It is used only to count requests and daily uploads, and is deleted within one day. We don't store your IP address itself.
  • Reports: the reason and any details you write, with the same scrambled code, so one person counts once. Reports are deleted together with the file.

In your browser

Your recent links and their delete keys are saved in your own browser (local storage), so you can delete a file early. They never leave your device unless you delete a file. We use no tracking cookies and no ads.

Services we use

  • Vercel runs the website and stores the files (in Frankfurt, Germany). Like any web host, it keeps short-lived technical logs.
  • Neon stores the link details listed above.
  • Google Fonts provides the site's font, so your browser contacts Google when a page loads.

Sharing

We don't sell or share your data. We only give information to authorities when the law requires it, and the little we keep is listed above.

Contact

Questions about privacy: use the report button on the file's page.
